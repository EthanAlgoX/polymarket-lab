The original LICENSE and copyright notice are retained.

Local modifications include:
- Event-based category discovery and a paginated market catalog with explicit coverage limits.
- A custom market research interface, market detail drawer, and shared responsive sidebar.
- Quote-age / opportunity gates, fail-closed fee handling, and an updated local cost model.
- Optional server-only DeepSeek translation, Chinese/English switching, and persistent translation caching.
- A macOS local launcher and redesigned monitor, tables, history, and settings views.
- Independent Decimal orderbook metrics, resilient public requests, revision-based catalog publication, and category-aware scanner selection.

Source-level research and independent implementation decisions for other projects are recorded in docs/OPEN_SOURCE_REVIEW.md, with commit-pinned links.

Other repositories listed in the research interface are references only; their code has not been incorporated.
