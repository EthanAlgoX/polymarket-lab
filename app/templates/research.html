Research directions

External feeds not connected · Models not backtested

Validate before recording a decision

  1. Read original settlement rules and verify mutually exclusive, exhaustive outcomes.
  2. Calculate common executable quantity, level-by-level cost, fees and buffers. Keep unknown inputs explicit.
  3. Review independent execution of both legs and unmeasured merge or on-chain costs.
  4. Retain the original data and validate predictive models out of sample before paper observation.

Weather observations, sports odds and Chainlink reference prices are not connected. These research directions do not establish a profitable edge in a specific market.

Source reviews document reusable ideas, adoption decisions and limitations. Stars are one maintenance signal; they do not validate returns or API compatibility.
