From research to a decision

  1. Check settlement rules, specified sources, and whether outcomes are mutually exclusive and exhaustive.
  2. Read executable depth and calculate tier-by-tier costs and fees for the common quantity of both legs.
  3. Deduct fees, slippage, safety buffers, and merge costs. Keep unverified fees marked as unknown.
  4. Record signals and results. Backtest weather, sports, and crypto predictions before paper observation.

Weather observations, sports odds, and Chainlink reference prices are not connected, and no predictive model has been backtested. These research directions and book estimates do not establish a profitable edge in a specific market.
